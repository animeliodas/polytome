# House style (all books, every target language except those with their own file in styles/)

This file is added to the translator prompt of every book. A book's own bible (.polytome/bible.md) may refine it.

- The text is meant both for reading and for listening (text-to-speech): smooth, easy-to-follow sentences.
- Numbers: keep digits that are digits in the original; write other numbers the way published fiction in the
  target language does.
- Money: convert the currency name to the usual target-language word (元/块 → yuan), keep the amount.
- Brand and product names (Porsche, iPhone) — in their usual spelling for the target language.
- Profanity: keep the emotion, soften it to what an audiobook allows.
- A character's gender — by pronouns and explicit cues of the original, not by behaviour or role.
